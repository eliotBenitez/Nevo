Kanban boards let you track tasks and projects as cards moving across columns, right inside your workspace.

## Usage

- Create a board with **New board** in the sidebar or on the Home screen.
- Add columns with **Add column**; rename or delete a column and set a **WIP limit** from its menu.
- Add cards with **Add card** or the quick-add field in a column, and drag cards between columns.
- Open a card to edit its notes, priority, progress, tags, custom fields (text, number, date, checkbox, select, multi-select) and links to other cards (blocks, blocked by, related).

## Views and filters

- Switch between **Board**, **Table** and **Calendar** views; the same cards appear in each.
- Use **Filter**, **Sort** and the search field to narrow cards down in any view.

## Automations and card templates

- **Automations** let you add rules such as "when a card enters a column, add a tag or move it", "when all subtasks are done", or "when a due date is near, send a notification".
- Any card can be saved as a **card template** to reuse its content, fields and priority.

## Notes

- Disabling the plugin only hides boards; your board data is preserved and comes back when you enable it again.
- Boards inside an imported archive are not merged into an existing workspace.
